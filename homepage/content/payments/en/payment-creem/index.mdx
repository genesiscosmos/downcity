---
title: Creem Payment Provider
---

Add `creemPaymentProvider()` to `PaymentService.providers`. Configure `CREEM_API_KEY`, `CREEM_PRODUCT_ID`, and optionally `CREEM_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.

```ts
await city.payment.method("creem").invoke({ credits: 5_000_000, amount_minor: 500, idempotency_key: "order_123" });
```

Use `on_paid(record)` to call `credits.topup()` idempotently with `payment:${record.payment_id}`.
