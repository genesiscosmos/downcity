---
title: Usage and Credits billing
description: Record usage facts, calculate prices in the product, and charge through Credits.
---

`UsageService` records model and service-call facts. It does not own pricing. Product logic calculates Credits from real usage and calls `credits.charge()`.

```ts
const ai = new AIService({ credits });

await credits.charge({
  user_id: "user_123",
  credits: calculate_credits(usage),
  source: "model_usage",
  ref: usage.request_id,
  idempotency_key: `model_usage:${usage.request_id}`,
  metadata: { model_id: usage.model_id },
});
```

The AIService Credits bridge checks minimum availability before execution and charges real usage afterward. Payment orders credit the Primary Card through `PaymentService.on_paid`; the lifecycles stay separate.
