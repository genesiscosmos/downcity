---
title: Waffo Payment Provider
---

Add `waffoPaymentProvider()` to `PaymentService.providers` and configure the merchant ID, private key, product ID, and webhook public key.

```ts
await city.payment.method("waffo").invoke({ credits: 5_000_000, amount_minor: 500, idempotency_key: "order_123" });
```

Issue Primary Card Credits idempotently from `on_paid(record)` after payment confirmation.
